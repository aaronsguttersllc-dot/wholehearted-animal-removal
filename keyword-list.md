# Keyword List — Wholehearted Animal Removal

Built from 2 real Semrush exports (2026-08-31): seed "Dead Animal Removal" (4,281 rows + 750 questions) and seed "Dead horse removal" (1,634 rows + 390 questions). Raw files kept in the project root for reference.

**Note on difficulty threshold:** unlike the gutters project's KD ≤30 cutoff, Aaron confirmed real local competition is essentially just 2 businesses (one ~30 min away near Graham, WA; one in Eastern Washington, not really local). Keyword Difficulty on Semrush mostly reflects competition from big national sites, not these two. Kept the ceiling loose here (up to ~KD 35-40) rather than the tighter gutters-site cutoff.

**Big caveat on the broad seed:** "Dead Animal Removal" as a Semrush seed pulled in enormous amounts of unrelated noise — recycling, junk removal, waste management, municipal "animal services" (live animal control, not this business) made up the vast majority of the 4,281 rows. Real, on-topic keywords were a small fraction. Filtered those out below; don't reuse the raw "All" export without re-filtering.

## Deprioritized

- **Rendering plant cluster** ("what is a rendering plant," "animal rendering," "rendering plant near me," etc.) — real search volume (up to 480/mo) but low relevance scores (mostly 27-29), likely mixed intent between homeowners and the industrial rendering business itself. Still worth its own blog post because of a real, genuinely differentiating fact from Aaron: **a rendering plant will not accept an animal that was euthanized** — euthanasia drug residue makes the carcass unsuitable for rendering. This is exactly the kind of situation this business exists for (a horse owner who's just had a vet euthanize their horse, then learns rendering isn't an option), and it's real, specific information most competitor content won't have. Good angle: "What Happens After a Horse Is Euthanized" or similar, explaining the real disposal options and why professional removal matters specifically in this case.
- **"dead cat" (5,400 vol, KD44)** — huge volume but broad/ambiguous intent (grief, pet loss content, not just removal-service searches). Possible pillar post, not a priority.
- **"animal removal" (1,300 vol, KD34)** — ambiguous: could pull in searches for *live* nuisance wildlife trapping (raccoon-in-the-attic type calls), which is a different, separately-regulated activity Aaron doesn't do. Don't use without further qualifying it (e.g., "deceased animal removal") so it doesn't misrepresent the service.
